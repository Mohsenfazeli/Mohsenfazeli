# Mohsen Fazeli

**Economics · Strategy · Decision Support · Financial Modelling · Project Delivery · Applied Analytics**

Economist at the Passenger Transportation Board in Victoria, BC · MA Economics (University of Victoria) · PMP

I turn financial, market and operational evidence into scenario models, forecasts and KPI frameworks that executives and Boards can act on.

## What I do

- **Financial and scenario modelling:** cost-driver and margin scenarios for rate setting; NPV, IRR and sensitivity analysis for investment feasibility.
- **Forecasting and planning:** multi-year demand and capacity frameworks; enrolment and workforce projections.
- **Decision support:** KPI frameworks, standard economic assessments, and briefings for Boards and executives.
- **Reproducible analytics and delivery:** tested data pipelines and dashboards built on official open data, plus scoping and cross-functional delivery of automation projects.
